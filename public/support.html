Common questions

How do I log a climb?
Tap the Log button in the center of the bottom bar, pick your climb type (boulder, top rope, or lead), how it went, and save. It lands in your logbook instantly.

Why do I need to be near my gym to log or set a home gym?
Klimb checks your location so your logbook reflects climbs you actually did at the gym. Make sure location access is enabled for Klimb in your device Settings.

I don't see my gym.
On the gym picker, use "Don't see your gym? Add it" to suggest one. New gyms are reviewed before they appear for everyone.

How do I switch between American and International grades?
Go to Profile → Settings and choose your grade system.

How do I delete my account?
Open Profile → Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
