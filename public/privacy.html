Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Klimb ("we," "us," or "the app") is a personal climbing logbook. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Klimb, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app:

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a display name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a basic identifier and (where you allow it) your name and email from that provider.
  • Your climbing content. The climbs you log — grades, outcomes, notes, ratings, projects, comments — along with any photos or videos you choose to add.
  • Home gym and location. Your selected home gym, and your device's approximate location at the moment you set a home gym, start visiting a gym, or log a climb. Location is used only to confirm you're near the gym. We do not track your location in the background or store a location history.
  • Social connections. Friend requests and connections you make, and your privacy choices for your logbook and projects.
  • Basic technical data. Standard information needed to operate and secure the service, such as authentication tokens.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and your logbook.
  • To calculate your stats, streaks, grade pyramids, and weekly recaps.
  • To verify that climbs and home-gym changes happen at a real gym you're near.
  • To power social features you opt into, like friends and shared climbs.
  • To keep the app secure and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How your information is shared

  • With other users, as you choose. Your display name and the sends, flashes, and projects you mark public may be visible to friends or other climbers. You control what's public in Settings, and projects can be kept private separately.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database, authentication, and file storage. Your data is processed on their infrastructure solely to provide the app.
  • Sign-in providers. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, those providers handle authentication under their own privacy policies.
  • Content you share. When you share a climb or recap to Instagram, Messages, or another app, that content is handled by the app you send it to.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile and personal data. Some routes you created may remain in the shared database in de-identified form so other climbers' logbooks stay intact, but they will no longer be linked to you.

Your choices

  • Set your sends and projects to public or private in Settings.
  • Turn location access on or off in your device Settings (some features require it).
  • Block other users, and report content that violates our guidelines.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time.

Children's privacy

Klimb is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated in the app.
