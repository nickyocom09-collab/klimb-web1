# Klimb App Store Screenshot Philosophy

The visual direction is **Night Route Atlas**: a premium, nocturnal climbing journal that combines the precision of a route guide with the momentum of an athletic app. Deep black-green fields, thin topographic contours, and restrained electric-green accents give every panel the same immediate identity without competing with the product itself. The atmosphere should feel adventurous and technical, but never busy.

The hierarchy is deliberately simple. Each panel begins with a small circular line icon, followed by a short two-line promise in oversized, tightly set type. White establishes the action while Klimb green lands the memorable payoff. Supporting copy stays to one or two short lines so a customer understands the benefit before studying the phone.

Real product captures are always the hero. Screens are presented inside a dark, softly reflective device frame, enlarged enough that genuine interface details remain legible. The device can tilt slightly to create energy, but its contents must never be redrawn, invented, or warped. Shadows and green edge light separate it from the background while preserving the near-black palette.

Decorative elements behave like route-map annotations. Subtle contour lines, sparse dotted travel paths, and low-contrast climbing terrain add depth around the phone rather than behind important text. They should reward a second look while disappearing at thumbnail size, keeping the screenshots clean and credible.

Across devices, the artwork uses a protected central composition instead of stretching. iPhone canvases favor a tall, immersive crop; iPad canvases preserve the same hierarchy within a wider field and allow additional breathing room around the device. The result is one coherent campaign across every App Store slot: direct, athletic, polished, and unmistakably Klimb.
